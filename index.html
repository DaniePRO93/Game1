what's in the notebook

5 seconds of slow-mo

Slow the world to a crawl and weave through ink bullets. Only kills refill it.

Eraser sword

A giant pencil with a pink blade. Lunge in and rub doodles out from the top down.

Slide & double jump

Slide with C, jump out of it to keep your speed, flick a second jump mid-air.

Sticky-note upgrades

Pick one after every wave: ricochet shots, ink bombs, bullet-swatting swords, extra jumps…

Combos & the clock

Chain kills for up to ×5 score. Beat the wave clock for bonus points and a 4th upgrade.

Draw the boss too

A second page for The Doodler. Make it as horrible as you like.
