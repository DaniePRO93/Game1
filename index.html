what's in the notebook

Your doodle, your enemy

Whatever you draw becomes the horde. Big, filled-in blobs are slow tanks. Thin stick figures are fast but fragile. The top quarter of the drawing is the headshot zone.

5 seconds of slow-mo

Hold Shift to slow the world to a crawl and dodge the ink bullets. The bar only refills when you erase enemies.

Eraser sword

A giant pencil with a pink eraser blade. Lunge in and rub enemies out from the top down.

Slide & double jump

Slide with C, jump out of it to keep your speed, then flick a second jump in mid-air.

Sticky-note upgrades

Pick an upgrade after every wave: ricochet shots, ink bombs, a sword that swats bullets, extra jumps, and more.

Combos & the clock

Chain kills for up to ×5 score. Beat the wave clock for bonus points and a 4th upgrade choice.

Draw the boss too

Every 5th wave, The Doodler shows up as whatever horror you sketched on the boss page.

Share a challenge

Send a friend a link with your doodles and your score. They fight your monsters and try to beat it.
